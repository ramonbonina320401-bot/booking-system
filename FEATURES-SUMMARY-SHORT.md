# Booking System — Quick Features

**Booking** — Online booking ng rooms/equipment na may calendar, walang double-booking, at live updates.

**Accounts** — May user signup/login, may 2 roles: Regular User at Admin.

**Admin Panel** — Dashboard na may charts, approve/cancel ng bookings, manage resources, at search/filter.

**Pending Approvals** — May notification bell ang admin, may live count at alert kapag may bagong booking.

**Dynamic Branding** — Pwedeng baguhin ng admin ang logo, kulay, at app name nang walang code change.

**Maintenance Mode** — One-click na i-block ang mga user habang naka-maintenance, may custom message.

**Mobile App** — Installable sa phone (PWA), gumagana offline, responsive sa lahat ng device.

**Theme** — May Light at Dark mode.

**Mabilis at Makinis** — Mabilis mag-load, may loading skeletons, animations, at madaling gamitin.
